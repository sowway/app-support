Thank you for using Bible Memorization Together. All memorization content is provided in Korean. If you have questions or experience an issue, please review the information below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. My memorization progress disappeared.
Progress stored on your device may be removed if you uninstall the app or reset your device. Because the app does not require an account, progress may not transfer automatically when you change devices.

Q. Audio does not play.
Check your device media volume and whether another app is playing audio. If the issue continues, contact us with your device model, OS version, and app version.

Q. Does the app show ads?
This app may display banner ads provided by Google AdMob.

Q. Do I need an account?
No. You can use the app without creating an account or signing in.

How to Delete Data

Memorization counts, dates, and your personal verses are stored only on your device. Deleted data cannot be recovered, so save anything you need before deleting it.

Android
In your device settings, go to Apps (or App management) > Bible Memorization Together > Storage, then select Clear data or Clear storage. Menu names and locations may vary by device and Android version.

iPhone or iPad
Touch and hold the app icon on the Home Screen, then choose Remove App > Delete App. You can also go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage, select the app, and tap Delete App. Choose Delete App, not Offload App, to remove the app's data stored on your device as well.
