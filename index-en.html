01 / OUR VISION

Every culture holds
new possibilities.

We discover new ways to share the Gospel within the culture God has entrusted to us.

Culture includes art, music, film, and literature. Today, games and digital culture also hold an important place in our lives. We believe these forms of culture can be more than entertainment or consumption: they can carry messages of goodness and the Gospel.

Games bring film, music, art, storytelling, and technology together. They offer new ways to connect because people do not just watch a story unfold; they experience it and take part in it.
