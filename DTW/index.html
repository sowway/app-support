Three Warriors of David Support

Three Warriors of David

Three Warriors of David is a casual roguelite game. If you have questions or run into a problem, please review the support information below.

Versions

Three Warriors of David is available in a standard version and a Pro version. The Pro version has no ads and lets you earn more currency; everything else is the same as the standard version. Both versions share this support page and the same Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What kind of game is it?
Three Warriors of David is a casual roguelite game.

Q. How is the Pro version different?
The Pro version shows no ads and lets you earn more currency. The game content and data handling are otherwise the same.

Q. What data does the app store?
The app handles only game save data and does not collect personal information. Save data is stored on your device.

Q. My save data is missing.
Your save data may be deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data from your device. Save data may not transfer automatically when you change devices.

How to Delete Data

Game save data is stored on your device. Deleted data cannot be recovered, so save anything you need before deleting it.

Android
In your device settings, go to Apps (or App management) > Three Warriors of David > Storage, then select Clear data or Clear storage. Menu names and locations may vary by device and Android version.

iPhone or iPad
Touch and hold the app icon on the Home Screen, then choose Remove App > Delete App. You can also go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage, select the app, and tap Delete App. Choose Delete App, not Offload App, to remove the app's data stored on your device as well.
