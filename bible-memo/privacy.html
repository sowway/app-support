Privacy Policy

Bible Memorization Together

You can use this app without signing up or logging in. Unless otherwise stated, your learning records are stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Use

This app may collect and use the following information:

  • App usage statistics, error logs, and device and operating system information
  • Advertising identifiers used to provide ads
  • Your email address and inquiry when you contact us

This information is used only to operate the app reliably, identify errors, improve the service, provide ads, and respond to inquiries.

2. Learning Data Stored on Your Device

Learning data you create or enter in the app, such as memorization counts, dates, and your personal verses, is stored only in local storage on your device and is not transmitted externally.

  • The learning data may also be deleted if you uninstall the app or delete its data from your device.
  • For instructions, see “How to Delete Data” on the app's support page.

3. Social Networking Features

This app does not provide social networking features such as social sign-in, adding friends, sharing posts, comments, or messaging. Therefore, it does not collect account or social platform information for social integration.

4. Advertising

This app may display Google AdMob banner ads. When the app is first launched, it asks whether the user is 13 years old or younger. If the user answers yes, the app displays child-directed, non-personalized ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and similar data to provide and measure ads.

For more information about ad-related data processing and personalized ad settings, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy

5. Audio Features

In-app audio playback is provided for your learning convenience and does not require a separate login or personally identifying information. Audio playback history is not stored on a server or transmitted to third parties.

6. Children's Personal Information

When the app is first launched, it asks whether the user is 13 years old or younger. The answer is stored locally in the device's player preferences and is not transmitted to the operator's servers or third parties. If a parent or guardian has questions about the processing of a child's personal information, please contact us using the information below.

7. Retention and Deletion

Inquiry information is deleted without undue delay after the purpose of processing has been fulfilled, unless applicable law requires it to be retained for a specified period.

Learning data stored on your device is deleted from the device when you uninstall the app or delete the app's data in your device settings.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or restriction of its processing.

  • You can delete learning data stored on your device by following the instructions on the support page.
  • For information held by the operator, such as an email address provided in an inquiry, you may contact us using the details below.
  • We will promptly review your request and take the necessary action.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other complaints, please contact us:

  • Support page: View How to Delete Data

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised due to changes in laws, the service, or data processing practices. We will notify you of material changes through an in-app notice or this page.
